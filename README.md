# Forest Hill Tea Smart Dashboard and Predictive Analytics System

Sri Lanka has a long history of producing high-quality Ceylon tea. Alongside traditional tea production, specialty and artisanal teas provide opportunities for small-scale producers to create high-value products while supporting local communities and sustainable practices.

Forest Hill Tea Company, located in the Sabaragamuwa Province near Adam's Peak, produces handmade specialty teas while focusing on forest-friendly tea production, environmental sustainability, and community empowerment.

The company works with small-scale tea farmers and local communities and provides employment and income-generation opportunities, particularly for village women and smallholder farmers.

## Overview

As business activities, production, sales, farmer participation, community activities, and sustainability initiatives generate different types of information, there is an opportunity to bring these data into a centralized digital platform.

This project proposes the development of a smart dashboard and predictive analytics system for Forest Hill Tea Company. The system will combine interactive monitoring, historical analysis, future estimates, alerts, and reporting in one web-based platform.

## Dashboard Areas

The proposed system will provide dashboards for:

- Overall business performance
- Tea production
- Farmers and community impact
- Women and employment
- Environmental sustainability
- Sales and markets

The system will use historical data to provide estimates for future production, sales, and demand. These predictions will support better planning and data-driven decision-making.

The project will initially focus on understanding the existing processes and available data at Forest Hill Tea Company. A UI prototype will be developed and reviewed with the organization before implementing the complete system.

## Objectives

To develop a web-based dashboard and predictive analytics system that helps Forest Hill Tea Company monitor its tea production, sales, community impact, and sustainability activities while supporting future planning through data-driven predictions.

1. Develop a centralized dashboard for viewing important Forest Hill Tea business information.
2. Monitor tea production according to tea types, batches, periods, and other relevant factors.
3. Provide information about farmers, farmer contributions, and community impact.
4. Monitor women employment, local employment, and training activities.
5. Track environmental and sustainability-related activities.
6. Analyze sales and market performance.
7. Identify historical trends using interactive charts and visualizations.
8. Develop prediction models for future tea production, sales, and demand based on available historical data.
9. Provide alerts and insights that can assist management in identifying important changes or trends.
10. Provide reports that summarize business, community, and sustainability performance.
